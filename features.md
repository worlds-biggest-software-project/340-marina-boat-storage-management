## Cross-Cutting Feature Themes

### Table-Stakes Features

These capabilities are present in nearly every solution and any viable marina management platform must match them:

- **Slip/berth management** — visual marina maps, occupancy tracking, boat assignment, and availability status
- **Reservation and booking system** — seasonal and transient dockage scheduling, contract management, and availability checks
- **Billing and invoicing** — automatic invoice generation for slip rentals, utilities, and services, with payment collection
- **Customer records and portal** — customer contact information, communication history, secure portal with invoice viewing and payment
- **Work orders and service scheduling** — request intake, technician assignment, job tracking, and completion
- **Parts inventory and ordering** — parts tracking, stock level management, and supplier integration
- **Accounting integration** — general ledger, accounts payable/receivable, and financial reporting (often via QuickBooks)
- **Multi-user access with role-based permissions** — marina staff, technicians, managers, and accounting roles
- **Reporting and dashboards** — occupancy rates, revenue, profitability, and operational metrics
- **Customer communication** — email and SMS notifications for invoices, updates, and marketing

### Differentiating Features

Capabilities present in some solutions that provide competitive advantage:

- **Dynamic pricing for transient dockage** — yield management adjusting slip prices based on demand, seasonality, and availability (Dockwa, Storable)
- **Marketplace integration** — boater discovery network connecting vessel owners with transient dockage (Dockwa)
- **Mobile-first technician workflows** — offline-capable field service apps with photo/meter capture and real-time sync (Storable, DockMaster)
- **Digital signature and contract assembly** — marine-specific PDF generation and e-signature workflows (DockMaster)
- **Fuel dispenser integration** — automated fuel tank monitoring and sales integration (MarinaOffice, Sirius)
- **Utility metering automation** — automated meter reading and billing integration (MarinaOffice, Sirius)
- **Club membership context** — reciprocal access, vessel tracking, and member-facing apps (Clubessential)
- **Multi-facility operational models** — support for diverse facility types beyond traditional marinas (Harbour Assist)
- **Dealership integration** — boat sales, F&I products, and trade-in workflows (DockMaster)
- **Contactless payment options** — health/safety-focused payment processing (Clubessential)

### Underserved Areas / Opportunities

Gaps that represent genuine opportunities for differentiation:

- **Predictive maintenance scheduling** — no solution mentioned integrates engine hours, manufacturer schedules, or service history to proactively recommend maintenance; most are reactive scheduling
- **Environmental compliance tracking** — while EPA Clean Marina Programme is mentioned in context, no solution explicitly automates pump-out logging, waste oil tracking, or compliance reporting
- **Fuel inventory optimisation** — platforms track fuel sales but few predict demand and optimize delivery schedules to reduce carrying costs and stockouts
- **Spill and leak detection** — no solution mentioned integrates anomaly detection in fuel dispensing data to flag potential theft or environmental hazards
- **Guest experience personalisation** — platforms send communications but rarely tailor messages (arrival briefings, weather alerts, post-visit surveys) to vessel type, stay duration, or boater profile
- **Lithium battery management** — ABYC E-13 standard (2025) requires specific charging and storage protocols; no platform mentioned addresses lithium-specific workflows
- **Competitive pricing benchmarking** — marinas lack visibility into regional pricing benchmarks for slips, fuel, and services
- **Technician capacity forecasting** — platforms track current availability but don't forecast service demand or recommend shift staffing
- **Vessel compliance tracking** — registration, insurance, and certification expiry tracking per vessel
- **Integration with telematics and OEM data** — no platform mentioned taps vessel health data for proactive service opportunities

### AI-Augmentation Candidates

Manual or rule-based features where AI could provide meaningfully better results:

- **Dynamic slip pricing** — AI yield management optimizing transient slip prices based on demand forecasts, seasonality, local events, and competitor availability
- **Predictive boat maintenance** — time-series forecasting of maintenance needs based on vessel age, engine hours, historical service patterns, and OEM schedules
- **Fuel demand forecasting** — time-series models predicting fuel consumption based on seasonal patterns, boater demographics, and event calendars to optimize delivery and inventory
- **Guest experience personalisation** — AI-generated arrival briefings, weather alerts, and post-visit surveys calibrated to vessel type, boater profile, and stay duration
- **Lead scoring and assignment** — AI classification of booking inquiries by likelihood to convert (transient vs. seasonal) and assignment to marina staff
- **Environmental compliance automation** — automated logging of pump-out station usage, waste oil collections, and spill incidents with compliance report generation
- **Technician capacity optimisation** — AI forecasting of service demand and recommending shift staffing and technician skill allocation
- **Pricing and cost optimisation** — analysis of labour costs, parts margins, and service demand to recommend pricing adjustments by service type
- **Vessel health monitoring** — integration of telematics and OEM data to flag maintenance needs before customer contact
- **Churn prediction** — analysis of usage patterns to identify at-risk customers for retention outreach

## Legal & IP Summary

All platforms analysed are proprietary commercial software with subscription-based licensing. No open-source marina management solutions were identified, limiting licensing compatibility concerns. Environmental compliance automation (EPA Clean Marina Programme, ABYC standards, USCG regulations, OSHA hazard communication) is not patented and represents an opportunity for differentiation. However, any compliance automation must carefully follow regulatory guidance and anticipate regulatory change; counsel specializing in marine environmental compliance should be engaged during implementation.

Fuel dispenser integration and utility metering integrations typically involve third-party hardware APIs (manufacturer-specific); legal review is recommended to ensure data flow compliance and liability boundaries. Sirius Marina's fuel and utility metering integrations (European origin) may involve GDPR or other data protection requirements if deployed internationally.

No material was omitted due to uncertainty during this research.

## Recommended Feature Scope

Based on the above analysis, a prioritised feature scope for a competitive marina and boat storage management platform:

**Must-have (MVP)**
- Visual slip/berth management with occupancy tracking and drag-and-drop assignment
- Reservation and booking system (seasonal and transient) with contract management
- Automated billing and invoicing for slip rentals, utilities, and services
- Customer portal with invoice viewing, payment, and booking requests
- Work order management with technician assignment and mobile job tracking
- Parts inventory tracking with automated reorder points
- Accounting integration (GL, AR/AP, financial reporting; QuickBooks or native)
- Role-based access control (marina staff, technician, manager, accounting)
- Real-time reporting (occupancy, revenue, profitability by department)
- Customer communication (email, SMS for invoices and notifications)

**Should-have (v1.1)**
- Mobile-first technician app with offline capability (photo upload, meter readings, job completion)
- Dynamic pricing for transient dockage with yield management optimization
- Fuel management with dispenser integration and sales tracking
- POS system for retail, fuel, and service payments
- Customer portal with booking request workflow for haul-outs and repairs
- Environmental compliance tracking (pump-out logging, waste oil, spill reporting)
- Maintenance inspection workflows with photo documentation
- Email and SMS marketing campaigns
- Multi-location support for marina groups with centralized reporting
- Utility metering integration (water, electricity, pump-out tracking)

**Nice-to-have (backlog)**
- Marketplace integration connecting boaters with marina dockage (network effects)
- Predictive maintenance scheduling using service history and OEM data
- AI-driven dynamic slip pricing with competitor analysis
- Fuel demand forecasting with automated delivery scheduling
- Guest experience personalization (arrival briefings, weather alerts, surveys)
- Lead scoring and assignment automation
- Lithium battery-specific workflows and charging station management
- Vessel compliance tracking (registration, insurance, certification expiry)
- Technician capacity forecasting and shift optimization
- Integrated accounting system (no separate QuickBooks) with payroll
- Competitive pricing benchmarking dashboard
