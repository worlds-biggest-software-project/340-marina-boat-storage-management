## Relevant Industry Standards

- **ABYC (American Boat & Yacht Council) Standards** – 65th supplement published in 2025, covering E-11 (AC & DC Electrical Systems), E-13 (Lithium Ion Batteries), and H-24 (Gasoline Fuel Systems); marina service departments must follow these for haul-out and repair work.
- **USCG Recreational Boating Regulations** – US Coast Guard standards for new recreational boats and marina facilities; ABYC-USCG joint certification programme launched in 2025 for marine industry professionals.
- **NMMA Certification Standards** – National Marine Manufacturers Association voluntary certification aligned with ABYC standards; relevant for marinas selling new boats.
- **EPA Clean Marina Program** – Voluntary best-management-practices programme covering fuel spill prevention, sewage pump-out, waste oil collection, and stormwater management; widely recognised by state environmental agencies.
- **OSHA Hazardous Waste and Marine Coatings** – Anti-fouling paint application and hull maintenance work triggers OSHA and EPA regulations on lead, copper, and volatile organic compounds.
- **State Waterways Authority Regulations** – Slip rental agreements, liveaboard policies, and fuel dock operations are subject to state-level harbour master and environmental agency requirements varying by jurisdiction.

## Market Research

Marina management software is a niche but growing segment driven by increasing recreational boating participation, aging marina infrastructure requiring digital modernisation, and the complexity of managing multiple revenue streams (wet slips, dry stack, fuel, guest dockage, boat maintenance, retail). Storable has emerged as the leading consolidator, having acquired Molo to build an all-in-one cloud platform strategy. Dockwa operates a dual-sided marketplace connecting transient boaters with marinas, generating commission revenue while providing guest-dockage management tools. Industry data indicates marinas relying on manual systems miss approximately 34% of enquiries during peak season, translating directly to empty slips and lost fuel revenue. Lithium battery adoption aboard recreational vessels (tracked in the 2025 ABYC E-13 standard update) is creating new marina infrastructure and service requirements. The market is highly fragmented geographically, with a large proportion of marinas still operated by municipalities, yacht clubs, or small private owners who have historically resisted software investment but are increasingly pressured by competitive and regulatory demands.

## AI-Native Opportunity

- **Dynamic slip pricing and transient yield management** – Demand-responsive pricing for transient guest dockage that adjusts based on seasonal patterns, local events, available slip inventory, and boater browsing behaviour—similar to hotel revenue management.
- **Predictive boat maintenance scheduling** – AI analysis of service history, engine hours, and manufacturer schedules across vessels stored at the marina to proactively recommend and schedule maintenance before failures occur.
- **Fuel inventory and spill prevention optimisation** – AI-monitored fuel tank levels with demand forecasting to optimise delivery schedules, combined with anomaly detection in dispensing data to flag potential leaks or theft early.
- **Guest experience and communication automation** – AI-generated personalised arrival briefings, weather alerts for berthed vessels, and post-visit follow-up surveys calibrated to the boater's vessel type and stay duration.
- **Environmental compliance monitoring** – Automated logging of pump-out station usage, waste oil collections, and fuel spill incidents against EPA Clean Marina programme requirements, with AI-generated compliance reports for annual certification reviews.
